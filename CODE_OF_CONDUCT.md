# Code of Conduct

Headroom is a small project, and we want working on it to be pleasant for everyone.

## Expectations

- Treat other people with courtesy, in issues, pull requests, and any other project space.
- Disagree about ideas and code, not about people. Stay on the technical point.
- Accept that maintainers have the final say on scope and direction.

## Not Acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Publishing someone's private information
- Spam or repeated off-topic posts

## Reporting

Report a problem through GitHub's private reporting on this repository (the **Security** tab, then
**Report a vulnerability**), or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled privately.

## What Happens Next

Maintainers review each report and may respond with a warning, remove content, or block a person from the
project. Decisions are made at the maintainers' discretion.
